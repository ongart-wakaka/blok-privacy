Privacy Policy

BLOK is an Android home-screen launcher made by Wakaka ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: almost everything stays on your phone. We have no servers, no user accounts, no analytics of our own and no ads.

1. Information that leaves your phone

Approximate location, for the weather. To show the weather, BLOK sends your approximate location, rounded to about 1 km (two decimal places), to Open-Meteo, a free weather service. This happens about every 30 minutes while weather is turned on. We never see this location ourselves. Open-Meteo's handling of requests is described in their terms. You can deny location access; the weather panel will then not show local weather.

Purchases (Google Play Billing). BLOK Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment. The app only receives whether you have bought Pro; we never receive your card or payment details. Google's Play Billing library, which is built into the app, sends Google technical logs about how the purchase system is working (for example, whether a request succeeded or failed, and connection problems) so Google can improve it and fix errors. These logs are handled under Google's Privacy Policy. BLOK has no server of its own and does not receive this data.

Replies you send. If you reply to a message from BLOK's notifications page, BLOK hands your text to the messaging app through that app's own reply button, the same way the notification shade does. The messaging app sends it; BLOK keeps no copy.

Apart from the weather request, Google Play Billing, Android's own backup (section 3) and messages you choose to send, BLOK sends nothing off your phone.

3. Backups

BLOK can export your layout and settings to a file. The file is created only when you ask, saved where you choose, and never uploaded by BLOK.

Android may also back up BLOK's layout and settings (pages, folders, names you give apps, albums, MY BLOK layouts) to your Google account and restore them on a new phone. This is Android's own backup, encrypted by Google, and you can turn it off in Android's settings. Notification and call content, your location and weather, picked contacts, photos and videos, and your Pro purchase and trial are never included.

4. Deleting your data

Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling BLOK, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from your phone, except songs you imported into the Music/BLOK folder, which stay as normal music files. Your Android backup can be deleted in your Google account's backup settings.

5. Children

BLOK is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

ข้อความตอบกลับที่คุณส่ง ถ้าคุณตอบข้อความจากหน้าแจ้งเตือนของ BLOK ข้อความจะถูกส่งต่อให้แอปแชตผ่านปุ่มตอบกลับของแอปนั้นเอง เหมือนการตอบจากแถบแจ้งเตือน แอปแชตเป็นผู้ส่ง BLOK ไม่เก็บสำเนา

นอกจากการขอข้อมูลสภาพอากาศ, Google Play Billing, การสำรองข้อมูลของ Android (ข้อ 3) และข้อความที่คุณเลือกส่งเอง BLOK ไม่ส่งข้อมูลใดออกจากเครื่อง
